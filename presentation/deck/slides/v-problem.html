The problem

One role. 1,000 CVs.

More than 66 working days for one recruiter.

And a black-box AI can invent claims, hide bias — and be hijacked by text hidden in a CV.

1,000 ÷ 15 documented evaluations a day = 66.7 working days

Small HR teams get hundreds of applications per role. Evaluating a thousand properly takes one recruiter more than 66 working days. Handing that to a black-box AI is risky: invented facts, hidden bias, and CVs can carry hidden instructions.
