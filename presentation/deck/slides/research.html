Prompt Cache

Reusing precomputed attention for repeated prompt segments — 1.5–10× faster time-to-first-token on GPU.

We apply: one shared job-and-rubric prefix, cached across every CV of a job.

Talent Detective · 09 · arXiv:2305.05176 · arXiv:2311.04934

Our cost design follows published research. FrugalGPT from Stanford shows that prompt adaptation and caching can cut LLM cost dramatically; we use compact structured prompts and reuse saved evaluations. Prompt Cache shows the value of reusing a shared prompt prefix; every CV for a job shares one cached job-and-rubric prefix. Re-ranking costs nothing at all.
